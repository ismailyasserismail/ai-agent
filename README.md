## StepUp Profile Website Prompt

Here is a ready-to-paste English prompt for Codex to design a StepUp profile website inspired by wipbdr.com:

---

**Prompt for Codex (Website / Profile Design for StepUp):**

You are a senior web designer and front-end developer.
Design and build a modern one-page company profile website for a digital marketing agency called **“StepUp – Digital Marketing Solutions”** based in the UAE and KSA.

### 1. Overall Style & Layout

* Visual style similar to [https://www.wipbdr.com](https://www.wipbdr.com) (dark, minimal, premium, futuristic).
* Full-screen sections, smooth scroll, responsive layout.
* Main background: solid black or very dark gray.
* Accent color: teal / cyan for highlights, buttons, circles, and hover states.
* Use a clean geometric sans-serif font (similar to Gilroy / Montserrat / Futura).
* Plenty of whitespace, strong typography, minimal lines and shapes.
* Add a few subtle abstract shapes or circular nodes behind sections, like a tech/creative studio.

### 2. Header & Navigation

* Fixed top navigation with logo on the left (StepUp logo placeholder) and links on the right:

  * Home, About, Services, Industries, Work, Contact.
* Transparent header that turns slightly darker on scroll.

### 3. Hero Section (Above the Fold)

* Dark background with a large teal circular shape on one side.
* Big bold headline:
  “We Turn Brands Into Digital Powerhouses.”
* Subheading text:
  “StepUp is a fast, ROI-driven digital marketing and tech partner for ambitious brands in the UAE and Saudi Arabia.”
* Primary CTA button: “Start Your Growth” (scrolls to Contact).
* Secondary CTA button: “See Our Services”.
* Optional: subtle looping animation, gradient, or glow around the circle.

### 4. “Who We Are” / About Section

* Short paragraph describing StepUp:
  “StepUp is a UAE-based digital marketing and technology agency specializing in performance marketing, creative content, web development, and automation. We blend creativity, data, and engineering to help brands grow faster in GCC markets.”
* 3–4 key stats (e.g. Years experience, projects delivered, industries, platforms).
* Minimal icon set, line-style.

### 5. Services Section (Cards / Grid)

Create a clean 2x3 or 3x3 grid of service cards with icons:

1. Performance Marketing (Google, Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, YouTube)
2. Social Media Management & Content
3. Creative & Branding
4. Website & Landing Page Development
5. Marketing Automation & CRM
6. Project Administration & Reporting

Each card should include:

* Service title
* Short 2–3 line description
* Small icon at the top
* Hover effect (slight scale + teal border or glow).

### 6. Unique Value Proposition Section

Two-column layout:

* Left: heading “Why StepUp?” with bullet points:

  * Fast execution and agile delivery
  * GCC cultural and language insight (Arabic + English)
  * Performance-focused, data-driven decisions
  * In-house content and media production
  * Marketing + Tech in one partner (ads, web, automation, CRM)
* Right: abstract tech diagram or circle node graphic showing “Content → Ads → Landing Page → CRM → Automation”.

### 7. Industries Section

* Dark background with lighter cards or icons.
* Title: “Industries We Serve”.
* Grid with icons and labels: Domestic Services, Real Estate, E-commerce & Fashion, Restaurants & Cafés, Clinics & Medical, Corporate & B2B, Technology Startups, Education & Training.

### 8. Tech Stack / Platforms Section

* Horizontal strip section with logos of platforms & tools in white/gray:
  Google, Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, WordPress, Shopify, Canva, Adobe (Ps, Ai, Pr, Ae), CRM, Automation, AI tools.
* Title: “Platforms & Tools We Work With”.

### 9. Process Section (Optional)

* Simple 4-step process bar:

  1. Discover
  2. Strategy
  3. Build & Launch
  4. Optimize & Scale
* Each step in a circle with a teal line connecting them.

### 10. “Work / Case Study” Preview (Optional)

* Section title: “Selected Work”.
* 3–4 project tiles (placeholder images) with overlay text on hover (client name + result like “+42% ROI”).

### 11. Contact / CTA Section

* Dark section with large centered text:
  “Ready to Step Up Your Brand?”
* Short line: “Tell us about your project and we’ll come back with a clear growth plan.”
* Contact form fields: Name, Company, Email, Phone, Budget range dropdown, Message.
* WhatsApp or fast-contact button.

### 12. Footer

* Left: StepUp logo and short description.
* Center: Navigation links repeated.
* Right: Social icons (Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok).
* Tiny text: “© StepUp Digital Marketing Solutions – UAE / KSA”.

### 13. Technical Requirements

* Clean, semantic HTML and modern CSS (or Tailwind if supported).
* Fully responsive (desktop, tablet, mobile).
* Smooth scrolling and minimal animations using CSS or lightweight JS only.
* Organize styles clearly so the colors and fonts can be easily changed.

Design the layout and code so the result visually feels as premium and minimal as wipbdr.com, but with StepUp’s name, services and messaging.

---
